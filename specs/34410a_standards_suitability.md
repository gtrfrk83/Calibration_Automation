## What was compared

* **TAR** (test accuracy ratio) = half-width of the 34410A pass/fail limit at the point / the calibrator's published 1 year accuracy at that output (the existing `tartur` definition). **TUR** = the same limit / the reported expanded uncertainty (k = 2) of the existing uncertainty budget (`uncertainty_budget`, lab scope CMC floor).
* **Limit column.** The Service Guide (p. 64) asks for an accuracy better than 1/5 of the instrument's **24 hour** specification, i.e. a TAR of 5:1 against the 24 hour limit; the 24 hour limits are used only within 24 hours of an adjustment (p. 66). The procedure itself judges against the **1 year** limits by default, so the ratios against the 1 year limit are shown as well. 4:1 and 1:1 are shown as the lower tiers.
* **5560A**: figures are the 1 year, **95 % (k = 2)** column, the column the laboratory's standard sheet holds. The 5560A guarantees its performance at the **99 % (k = 2.58)** level (5560_Specs.pdf p. 1); the 99 % TAR is given in the appendix. Resistance uses the floor that assumes an ohms zero within 24 hours (+/-1 C); with only a 14 day zero the floor is much larger (appendix).
* **5522A**: the laboratory's own sheet; its note 8 says four 1 year rows are deliberately more conservative than the manual (DC current 329.999 mA and 1.1 to 2.99999 A, resistance 11 to 33 MOhm and 110 to 330 MOhm). Used as stored. The sheet states no confidence level.
* **5520A: UNVERIFIED.** No 5520A specification is on file. The 5522A rows stand in for it (the 5522A is the successor with the same core ranges), so the 5520A column is a proxy, not a result. ## Recommendation

1. **Use the 5560A as the source for the whole procedure.** It can source every point (the 5522A and the 5520A proxy cannot source three AC volts points at 300 kHz) and its TAR is higher than the 5522A's at every point except #65 (AC volts 10.00 V @ 10.00 Hz); #87 (AC volts 1.000 V @ 10.00 Hz).
2. **Against the 1 year limits** (what the procedure applies by default) the 5560A gives a worst-case TAR of 4.8:1 for DC volts, 4.8:1 for DC current, 4.8:1 for resistance and 28.0:1 for frequency: at least 4:1 everywhere there, with 5:1 met at most of those points (see the tables). The 5522A (and the 5520A, if it matches) reaches 2.4:1 for DC volts, 1.4:1 for DC current and 3.0:1 for resistance: usable for a 1:1 / 2:1 comparison, not 4:1.
3. **Against the manual's own 24 hour column nobody reaches 5:1** except frequency (20.0:1) and the 2-wire 100 MOhm point on the 5560A. Best worst-case TAR of the two (24 hour): DC volts 2.6:1, DC current 1.4:1, resistance 1.2:1. The manual sizes its source (a 5720A) for the 24 hour specification, which applies only within 24 hours of an adjustment; for a calibration interval check the 1 year limits are the relevant ones.
4. **AC volts and AC current need an external standard (or a better AC source) to reach 4:1.** The 5560A's worst-case 1 year TAR is 1.2:1 for AC volts and 0.6:1 for AC current; the 5522A's is 0.6:1 and <0.1:1. AC current is below 1.2:1 even on the 5560A, so it is a measurement against a calibrator at least as uncertain as the limit: report the TUR, or read the calibrator output with a better AC measurement standard (the suite already has 5790B support) and use that reading as the reference.
5. **Frequency**: the 2.5 ppm of both calibrators is 0.1 mHz at 40 Hz and 25 mHz at 10 kHz, against limits of 20 mHz and 0.5 Hz (24 hour): no external frequency standard is needed for these two points.
6. **High resistance**: the 5560A covers 10 MOhm (4-wire) and 100 MOhm (2-wire) with room to spare; the 5522A (and the 5520A proxy) is at 3.0:1 (10 MOhm, 4-wire) against the 1 year limit, 15:1 at 100 MOhm, and its lab-sheet floors for 11 to 330 MOhm are deliberately more conservative than the manual (5522A sheet note 8). No external standard is needed with the 5560A.
7. **AC bandwidth**: the 5522A / 5520A sheets stop before 10 V at 300 kHz, 50 V at 300 kHz and 70 V at 300 kHz (points 68, 71, 74); the 5560A covers them (70 V maximum above 100 kHz on its 120 V range). If only a 5522A / 5520A is available, those three points need an external AC source.
8. **The 5520A is a proxy.** Replace it with real figures when its specification pages are supplied.
